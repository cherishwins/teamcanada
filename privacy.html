Privacy

The short version: we barely collect anything, and we sell nothing.

Team Canada (primestrength.ca) is a static information site. There are no accounts, no logins, and no tracking cookies. Last updated 2 June 2026.

Analytics

We use privacy-friendly, cookieless web analytics (Umami, and optionally Vercel/Cloudflare). They count page views and rough trends in aggregate. They do not set cookies, do not build a profile of you, and do not track you across other sites. That's why you won't see a cookie banner — there's nothing to consent to.

If you join the coalition

The form at /join runs entirely in your browser. When you hit submit, it opens your own email app with a pre-filled message to jesse@primestrength.ca. We only ever receive what you choose to send us — typically your business name, your email, a link, and a logo. We use it for one thing: to add your business to the coalition wall, with your permission. We don't sell it, rent it, or share it. Ask us to remove you any time and we will.

Server logs & hosting

The site is served by Vercel, fronted by Cloudflare. Like all web hosts, they keep short-lived technical logs (e.g. IP address, browser type) to deliver pages and block abuse. We don't use those logs to identify you.

Your rights

Under Canada's PIPEDA and Québec's Law 25, you can ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Because we hold so little — really just the coalition emails people send us — that's usually a one-line request. Email jesse@primestrength.ca.

Children

This site is general-audience civic information and isn't directed at children.
