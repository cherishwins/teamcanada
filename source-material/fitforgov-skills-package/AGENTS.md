# Fit For Gov — Agent Instructions

This repository carries the Fit For Gov brand, narrative, and persuasion system as
**skills** under `.fitforgov/skills/`. Before producing ANY Fit For Gov–branded
output — a page, an RFP response, a LinkedIn post, an email, an OG image, a v0
prompt — read the relevant skill file(s) first and follow them exactly.

A "skill" here is a folder containing a `SKILL.md` (instructions) plus optional
`references/` (deeper docs) and `assets/` (logo files). Read the SKILL.md fully,
then open any reference file it points you to.

## When to load which skill

| If the task is about… | Load this skill | Always also load |
|---|---|---|
| How it LOOKS or SOUNDS — colors, fonts, the seal, layout, voice, banned words | `.fitforgov/skills/fitforgov-brand-kit/SKILL.md` | — |
| What the practice CLAIMS TO BE — positioning, the story, the facts you may cite | `.fitforgov/skills/fitforgov-narrative/SKILL.md` | brand-kit |
| How the words SELL — making a reader pick up the phone, conversion, sales copy | `.fitforgov/skills/fitforgov-persuasion/SKILL.md` | brand-kit |

**Rule of thumb:** `fitforgov-brand-kit` is mandatory for every Fit For Gov surface.
Stack `fitforgov-narrative` on top when writing net-new content (so the facts and
positioning stay coherent). Stack `fitforgov-persuasion` on top when the goal is to
drive an inbound phone call rather than just inform.

## Non-negotiables (summary — the SKILL.md files are authoritative)

- Colors: Paper `#F5F3EE`, Ink `#141414`, Mid `#4A4A4A`, Rule `#CFC9BC`,
  Oxblood `#5A1418`, Copper `#B0622D`. Never pure white, never pure black.
- Fonts: Instrument Serif (display), Inter Tight (body), JetBrains Mono (data).
- `border-radius: 0` everywhere. No drop shadows, gradients, glassmorphism, emoji
  in brand voice, stock illustration, or AI hero imagery.
- Every page ends with the phone number: **+1 250 415 5678 · jesse@fitforgov.com**.
- Banned words include: leverage, seamless, robust, scalable, synergy, ecosystem,
  revolutionary, cutting-edge, solutions, partners, stakeholders, empower, unlock,
  transform, journey, dive in, deep-dive, game-changing, disruptive, innovative.
- The seal (`fitforgov-brand-kit/assets/logo-seal*.png`) is small and formal, never
  a hero element, never recolored.

## What these skills are NOT for

These are Fit For Gov only. Do not apply them to Cherish / VI Care, 주체강 (JucheKang),
Ibrahim Energy, or iPurpose. Each of those has its own identity.

## Verification

Before you call any Fit For Gov output done, re-check it against the brand-kit's
"Forbidden Treatments" table and "Banned Words" list. If a claim cites a number,
confirm the number against `fitforgov-narrative/references/canonical-facts.md`.
