# Propagation Engineering

The discipline of engineering truth for share.

## When to use

Trigger on: "make this travel", "why won't this spread", "engineer the share", "diagnose this post", "make it go", "narrative virality", "propagation", "viral mechanics", "why is this getting shared", "make it shareable", or any moment where Jesse has something true that needs to compete for attention against something false but better-packaged.

Also trigger on the inverse: any time Jesse is about to publish, before he posts. Run the check. The discipline lives in the pre-publish moment, not the post-mortem.

NOT for: propaganda, manipulation, manufactured claims, or "growth hacking" in the marketing sense. This skill assumes the underlying truth is already settled. It engineers form, not substance.

## Core thesis

An idea spreads when three forces line up at once: **high-arousal emotion** in the receiver, **confirmation of the receiver's tribal identity**, and **narrative architecture simple enough to survive retelling**.

Truth value is almost decorative to propagation. The machinery is neutral. Most truth currently in the world is losing the propagation war to better-packaged falsehood. The only honest response is to learn the machinery and apply it to true things, with discipline, knowing that every important truth has to compete in propagation channels that systematically reward simpler and more emotionally resonant stories regardless of accuracy.

## The three load-bearing forces

**1. High-arousal emotion (Berger).** Awe, anger, anxiety, hope. These are the emotions that compel an action, and sharing is an action. Low-arousal emotions — sadness, contentment, mild interest — suppress sharing because they don't produce the activation energy a share requires. If your message produces only low-arousal feelings, it will not move regardless of how important the underlying point is.

The corollary: sad news about important things does not travel. Outraged news about trivial things does. This is structural and it is not going to change.

**2. Identity-protective cognition (Kahan).** Receivers do not process information primarily for accuracy. They process it for what accepting it would signal about who they are and which group they belong to. If accepting your message would cost the receiver tribe membership, no amount of evidence will compel acceptance. If accepting it confirms tribe membership, they will share it before finishing the headline.

Intelligence amplifies this dynamic rather than correcting it. The smarter the receiver, the better they are at finding sophisticated reasons to reject information that threatens their tribal identity. Do not assume an educated audience is a critical audience.

**3. Narrative simplicity (Trivers).** A story with a clear villain, a clear victim, a clear hero, and a simple moral architecture will out-compete an accurate account that has none of those things. Human cognition evolved to prefer self-serving simple stories. Complexity is paid in shares. The more comfortable the simplification, the further the message travels.

The discipline: simplify only to the limit of intellectual honesty. If the simple version is genuinely wrong, do not simplify further. Find the truer simple version, or accept that the message will travel less far.

## The pre-publish diagnostic

Before publishing anything intended to travel, answer three questions out loud:

1. **What arousal does this produce in the target receiver?** Specify the emotion. "Interest" is not an answer. "Awe at the scale of misreading" is.

2. **What identity does accepting this message confirm?** Specify the tribe. "Smart people" is not an answer. "Canadians who are tired of apologizing for their resource industry" is.

3. **Can the core claim survive being retold by a friend at a dinner party in one sentence?** If the retelling requires three sentences, the form is wrong. Rework until it survives the dinner-party test, or accept that it will not travel.

If the answer to any question is "none" or "I don't know," the message will not move. Either rework it, accept that it is for the file and not for the feed, or publish it knowing it is the seed of a longer game rather than a candidate for immediate spread.

## Voice register crosswalk

Each of Jesse's voices is a propagation strategy tuned to a different combination of the three forces.

- **Sutter** — high-arousal sardonic, individual identity (sharp, no-bullshit operators who recognize their own). Best for posts where the receiver should feel pleasure at being told the unvarnished thing.
- **Letters from the Earth** — high-arousal cosmic, civilizational identity (people who already half-believe the species is in trouble). Best for moments where the share-impulse is "I have to show someone this" because the framing names something they couldn't name themselves.
- **iPurpose** — medium-arousal institutional, professional identity (the DM-and-forward audience inside organizations). Best for content that needs to travel through the screenshot-in-Slack channel rather than the public feed.
- **Salon** — high-arousal warm, peer-identity (the in-group recognizes its own). Best for comment-section work where the propagation is sideways rather than outward.
- **JPanda** — medium-arousal contemplative, species-identity (people who like Sagan, McLuhan, the long view). Travels slowly but durably. The audience is smaller and more loyal.

Pick the voice to match the receiver's identity-protective requirements and emotional threshold. Do not write Sutter for a JPanda audience or vice versa. The voice IS the propagation strategy. There is no "neutral" voice.

## Case applications

**The Addition Paradox (NPSI WP-04).** Arousal: awe at the scale of a fifteen-year misreading. Identity: Canadians tired of apologizing for their resource industry. Architecture: 858 + 314 = 1,172, four assets, five buyers, one hand to play. All three forces aligned, applied to a defensible claim. The paper traveled.

**"Russia is running out of missiles" (2022–2023).** Arousal: hope. Identity: Western anti-autocrat. Architecture: the enemy is weakening. All three forces present. Spread fast. Aged badly because the underlying claim was selectively true rather than robustly true. Same machinery, low-discipline application. A cautionary case.

**"Canadian oil is dirty" (~2010–2025).** Arousal: shame and outrage. Identity: climate-aware urban progressive. Architecture: oil = pollution = bad. Won the public discourse for fifteen years despite emissions-intensity data pointing the other way. A case study in how propagation can defeat accuracy when the accurate story is harder to package.

**Putin-has-cancer (2022).** Arousal: hope. Identity: Western intelligence consumer. Architecture: the autocrat is dying, the regime is brittle, the war ends soon. Spread through allied SIGINT briefings without ever being well-grounded. The classic case of motivated reasoning entering even disciplined channels because the form was right and the political weather was pushing the wishful direction.

## The discipline clause

This skill engineers form, not substance. The discipline is:

- Apply only to truths you would defend under hostile cross-examination.
- Track confidence level on the underlying claim *separately* from optimization for share. The two questions are independent.
- Never invent emotional content the underlying truth doesn't actually carry. The credibility leak compounds.
- Never invent identity confirmations the underlying truth doesn't actually support. The tribal lie eventually breaks and the person who told it goes with it.
- The simplification is the only place where compromise is allowed, and only to the limit of intellectual honesty.
- If you find yourself optimizing the propagation of something you know is not robustly true, stop. You have left this skill and entered propaganda. They are different disciplines.

## What this skill is NOT

- **Not propaganda.** Propaganda manufactures the underlying claim. This skill assumes the claim is already true and only engineers the form.
- **Not manipulation.** Manipulation hides the persuasive intent. This skill is transparent — anyone can run the same diagnostic on the same content and reach the same conclusion.
- **Not virtue.** The machinery has no morality of its own. The morality is supplied entirely by the truthfulness of the underlying claim and the integrity of the practitioner.
- **Not growth hacking.** Growth hacking optimizes for engagement metrics regardless of substance. This skill optimizes for fidelity-preserving spread of substance.

## The deeper rule

People share what makes them feel like they already are who they want to be, simply enough that the share doesn't require explanation, and emotionally enough that not sharing would feel like a failure of conviction.

Everything else is decoration.

If you can offer a receiver a true claim that lets them feel that way without compromising their integrity, they will carry your message further than any paid distribution. If you cannot offer them that, no amount of effort will move the message. The work is not in pushing harder. The work is in finding the truer version of the claim that fits this geometry without forcing.
